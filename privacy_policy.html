AxisMind — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

1. Introduction

AxisMind ("we", "us", "our") is a meditation and mindfulness application. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and the rights you have over your information.

By using AxisMind, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

2. Data Controller

The data controller for the processing described in this policy is Alex Merch, located at Tiraspol, Moldova. For any questions, contact us at alex.locaiciuc.dev@gmail.com.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Data you provide directly

  • Account data: email address and display name (when you sign in with Google or email/password);
  • Authentication identifier: a unique user ID (UID) generated by Firebase Authentication;
  • Profile and preferences: meditation goals, ranks, streaks, notification preferences, and language settings.

3.2 Data collected automatically

  • Usage data: meditation sessions, session duration, frequency, and timestamps;
  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system version, and app version, when needed for troubleshooting and improving the app;
  • Notifications: information related to reminders you enable (e.g., daily meditation reminders);
  • Analytics: aggregated usage statistics processed by Firebase Analytics (e.g., screen views, feature usage, device type);
  • Crash reports: crash logs and technical device information processed by Firebase Crashlytics to help us diagnose and improve app stability.
The app stores certain data locally on your device. When you sign in, relevant data (profile, progress, goals, statistics) is synchronized to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so you can access it across devices.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the application;
  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you;
  • To synchronize your data across your devices;
  • To send reminders and push notifications you have requested;
  • To track your progress, ranks, and meditation goals;
  • To personalize and improve your experience;
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve the app;
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect the security of our services.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide the services you requested;
  • Consent: for optional features such as certain notifications, where applicable;
  • Legitimate interests: to secure and improve the application, provided these interests are not overridden by your rights.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share data only with the following service providers that process it on our behalf:

  • Google LLC / Google Firebase — Firebase Authentication (account management), Cloud Firestore (data storage), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications). Firebase services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics — aggregated usage analytics and crash reporting, including technical device information, used to analyze usage and improve app stability.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of users and the public.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed on servers operated by Google in various countries, including the United States. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as Google's Standard Contractual Clauses, to ensure an adequate level of protection.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the service and fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Account and usage data are retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time, and we will delete it within a reasonable period, unless we are required to retain it by law.

9. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS) and restricted access to personal data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"): request deletion of your data;
  • Restriction: request limitation of processing;
  • Data portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell your personal information. You also have the right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at alex.locaiciuc.dev@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all associated data directly in the app: open Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, meditation history, progress, goals, ranks, and preferences from our servers.

Alternatively, you may request account and data deletion by sending an email to alex.locaiciuc.dev@gmail.com (or by submitting a web form if available). We will process your request within a reasonable period, typically up to 30 days.

11. Children's Privacy

AxisMind is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

12. Health Data and Mindful Minutes

AxisMind may track mindfulness metrics, such as minutes of meditation ("Mindful Minutes") and related statistics. Health-related data associated with your account is never sold, never shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes, and is never used for targeted advertising. If AxisMind ever integrates with Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect, such data will be used solely to display your meditation progress to you and will be subject to this same guarantee.

13. No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the application or by other appropriate means. The "Effective date" at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at: alex.locaiciuc.dev@gmail.com.
